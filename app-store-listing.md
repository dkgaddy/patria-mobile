## Description (4000 chars max)

Explore the complete biblical family tree — from the Creator to the Patriarchs, the Tribes of Israel, and beyond.

PATRIA is a beautifully crafted interactive genealogy of the Bible. Navigate thousands of years of scripture in a single, elegant tree. Tap any figure to read their biography, name meanings in Hebrew and Greek, scripture references, and their full web of relationships.

**Explore the Complete Tree**
Follow the lineage from YHVH through Adam, Seth, Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all twelve tribes. Trace the lines of kings, judges, prophets, and the genealogy of Jesus Christ. Every person is connected — follow relationships in any direction.

**Deep Biographical Detail**
Each figure includes:
• Name meanings in Hebrew and Greek with Strong's numbers
• Scripture references with full verse text
• Unique attributes and biblical notes
• Family relationships — fathers, mothers, spouses, children

**Powerful Search**
Search across 3,000+ biblical figures instantly. Find anyone by name, tribe, or description.

**Built for Study**
Tap any scripture reference to read the full verse inline. Use landscape mode to view the tree and a biography side by side.

**Free to Explore**
The lineage from Creation through Abraham is free. Unlock the complete tree — all 3,000+ figures from Genesis through Revelation — with a single one-time purchase.

Whether you're a student of scripture, a pastor, a parent teaching your children, or simply curious about the Word — PATRIA brings the biblical family to life in a way you've never seen before.
